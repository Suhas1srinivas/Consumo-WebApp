Data Science & Engineering

Using the Models: How Things Are Done Now

A rigorous algorithmic pipeline tailored for complex, multi-echelon retail environments.

LightGBM & CatBoost

Gradient-Boosted Ensembles

Handles millions of non-linear feature interactions simultaneously. Captures price shifts, holiday calendars, localized cannibalization, and store-level footfall dynamics with zero manual tuning.

MinT Reconciliation

Hierarchical Reconciliation

Mathematically reconciles SKU, Store, Regional Distribution Center (DC), and National aggregate forecasts using Minimum Trace (MinT) optimization. Guarantees 100% mathematical coherence across all business layers.

Croston’s & TSB

Intermittent & Lumpy Demand

Slow-moving and long-tail products suffer from zero-inflated demand. Specialized Teunter-Syntetos-Babai (TSB) models decouple demand timing from volume, eliminating severe buffer overstocking.

Continuous Sensing

Exogenous Feature Pipelines

Continuously ingests external weather forecasts, inflation indicators, social sentiment indices, and competitor shelf-pricing feeds to dynamically adjust short-term replenishment recommendations.

Temporal Attention

Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT)

Deep neural attention architectures model multiple forecast horizons simultaneously, identifying complex seasonal regimes and multi-week promotional hangover dips with high uncertainty calibration.

SHAP Explainability

Transparent Feature Attribution

No black-box predictions. Shapley additive explanations (SHAP) show planners the precise contribution of price (+8%), promo campaign (+22%), or seasonal temperature (-5%) driving every forecast.
