The Inventory Paradox: Too Much of What Doesn't Sell, None of What Does

Planners rely on static 30-day forward cover rules and fixed reorder points. In reality, supplier lead times fluctuate by weeks, and regional demand is volatile. Warehouses brim with stagnant inventory tying up capital, while high-velocity lines face devastating stock-outs and emergency air-freight fees.

Siloed Single-Echelon Buffers

Each distribution center and retail store calculates safety stock independently, stacking redundant buffers across the network.

Stochastic Lead-Time Blindness

Spreadsheet models treat 14-day vendor lead times as rigid constants, leaving companies vulnerable to supply chain shocks.

Dead Stock & Obsolescence

Capital sits frozen in slow-moving inventory while top-tier SKUs starve for space and financing.

Optimization Algorithms

Using the Models: How Inventory Is Managed Now

Mathematical constraint programming combined with Bayesian probability distributions.

Multi-Echelon MEIO

Network-Wide Pooling

Treats your supply chain as an interconnected ecosystem. Balances stock between Tier-1 central distribution hubs, regional DCs, and retail backrooms to pool risk and prevent localized stockouts.

Stochastic Bayesian Modeling

Lead-Time Uncertainty Distributions

Replaces static single-day lead-time assumptions with dynamic probability distributions. Automatically widens protective buffers when supplier delivery variability increases.

MILP Optimization

Constraint Programming

Solves Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) equations every night to balance working capital limits, warehouse pallet capacity, pallet order minimums (MOQs), and container fill rates.

Dynamic ABC-XYZ

Self-Healing SKU Segmentation

Automatically reclassifies SKUs weekly based on velocity and volatility. Moves fast-moving items into tight automated continuous replenishment cycles while assigning probabilistic buffers to long-tail items.

Shelf-Life Decay

Perishable & Expiry Prevention

Models batch expiration dates and degradation curves. Automatically issues transfer or bundle markdown triggers before SKUs cross into unrecoverable obsolescence zones.

OTIF Optimization

Service Level Targeting

Allows leadership to set tailored customer service level targets (e.g., 99.5% for strategic tier-1 retailers vs. 95% for bulk wholesale) and calculates the exact mathematical stock required.
