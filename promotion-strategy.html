The Promotion Trap: Subsidizing Consumers Who Would Buy Anyway

Consumer brands and retail chains dedicate up to 20% of gross revenue to trade promotions. Yet without causal AI modeling, most promotions simply give unneeded discounts to loyal buyers, generate deep forward-buying troughs that kill demand for weeks after, and cannibalize sibling products on the shelf.

The Illusion of Volume

Spreadsheets report high unit sales during the promo week, mistakenly celebrating a "success" that destroyed net category margin.

The Post-Promo Crash (Forward-Buying)

Shoppers stockpile discounted products during the promo, starving full-margin retail sales for the following 4 to 6 weeks.

Trade Spend Misallocation

Annual trade marketing budgets are blindly rolled forward year after year based on habit rather than algorithmic ROI evidence.
