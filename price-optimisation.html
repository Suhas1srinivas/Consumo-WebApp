The Pricing Dilemma: High Prices Kill Volume; Low Prices Destroy Margin

Commercial managers often adjust prices by blanket percentages (e.g. "+3% across the board") or enter margin-destroying price wars to match competitors. Without measuring true consumer price elasticity and category cannibalization, companies suffer painful volume collapse on sensitive items while leaving substantial margin untapped on price-inelastic essentials.

Blind Spot on Elasticity

Spreadsheets assume a flat relationship between price and volume, failing to identify non-linear demand drop-off cliffs.

Hidden Cannibalization

Discounting SKU A often cannibalizes higher-margin SKU B within the same category, resulting in net negative profit.

Competitor Knee-Jerk Matching

Price wars erode category profit pools without driving sustainable consumer loyalty or market share gains.
