The Core Challenge

Can Your Planning Keep Up With Your Business?

FMCG and Retail businesses generate enormous amounts of data across Sales, Inventory, and Supply Chains.

Fragmented data, disconnected systems and manual spreadsheets make it difficult to prevent stockouts and protect working capital.

01 / CHALLENGE

Demand Uncertainty

Changing customer demand, seasonality, and unrecorded stockouts make accurate forecasting difficult with spreadsheets.

02 / CHALLENGE

Excess Inventory & Stock-Outs

Too much inventory ties up working capital in warehouses. Too little leads to empty shelves, lost revenue, and poor service levels.

03 / CHALLENGE

Supplier & Lead-Time Volatility

Supplier MOQs, pack-size rules, and volatile transit lead times cause severe stock imbalances and costly bullwhip distortions.

04 / CHALLENGE

Fragmented ERP & POS Data

Critical signals across sales velocities, warehouse inventory, in-transit orders, and ERP ledgers remain disconnected.
